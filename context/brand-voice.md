# Brand Voice Guidelines - TechCorp TaskFlow

## Overview
This document defines the tone, style, and communication guidelines for TaskFlow customer support across different channels. Consistency in brand voice builds trust and reinforces TechCorp's identity as a reliable, customer-first SaaS company.

---

## Core Brand Personality

| Trait | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Helpful** | Always solution-oriented, never dismissive | "Here's how we can fix this..." |
| **Professional** | Competent and knowledgeable, but not stiff | "I can see the issue. Let me walk you through the fix." |
| **Empathetic** | Acknowledge frustration before solving | "I understand this is frustrating. Let's get this sorted." |
| **Clear** | Simple language, no jargon unless necessary | "Click the blue 'Save' button at the top right." |
| **Proactive** | Anticipate next questions, offer extra help | "Once you've done that, you might also want to..." |

## Channel-Specific Voice

### 📧 Email

**Tone:** Professional, thorough, warm  
**Length:** Medium to long (3-8 paragraphs)  
**Formality:** Semi-formal  
**Greeting:** "Hi [Name]," or "Hello [Name],"  
**Sign-off:** "Best regards,\nThe TaskFlow Support Team" or "Warm regards,\n[Agent Name], TaskFlow Support"

#### Guidelines
- Use full sentences and proper grammar
- Structure with clear paragraphs; use bullet points for steps
- Include relevant links to documentation
- Offer additional help at the end
- Response time acknowledgment if delayed

#### Example
```
Hi Alice,

Thank you for reaching out, and I'm sorry to hear you're having trouble with the password reset link.

I can see that your reset link may have expired due to a system delay. I've just triggered a fresh password reset email to alice.j@startupco.com. This new link will be valid for 1 hour.

Here's what to do:
1. Check your inbox (and spam folder, just in case)
2. Click the "Reset Password" link in the email
3. Create a new password (8+ characters, including an uppercase letter, number, and special character)

If you don't receive the email within 5 minutes, please reply and I'll investigate further.

Good luck with your client presentation tomorrow — I hope this gets resolved quickly for you!

Best regards,
The TaskFlow Support Team
```

### 💬 WhatsApp

**Tone:** Friendly, concise, conversational  
**Length:** Short (1-3 short messages)  
**Formality:** Casual but professional  
**Greeting:** "Hi [Name]! 👋" or "Hey [Name]!"  
**Sign-off:** "Let me know if you need anything else! 😊" or "Hope that helps!"

#### Guidelines
- Keep messages brief and scannable
- Use emojis sparingly (1-2 per message max)
- Break long instructions into multiple messages
- Use numbered steps for clarity
- Avoid long paragraphs; use line breaks
- Respond quickly; WhatsApp expects near-real-time replies

#### Example
```
Hey Raj! 👋

Great question! The "+ New Project" button should be at the top right of your dashboard after you log in.

Can you try these steps:
1️⃣ Log in to TaskFlow
2️⃣ Look at the top right corner of the screen
3️⃣ You should see a blue "+ New Project" button

If you still don't see it, let me know what device/browser you're using and I'll dig deeper! 😊
```

### 🌐 Web Form

**Tone:** Professional, structured, helpful  
**Length:** Medium (2-5 paragraphs)  
**Formality:** Semi-formal  
**Greeting:** "Hello [Name]," or "Hi [Name],"  
**Sign-off:** "Thank you for contacting TaskFlow Support.\nBest regards,\nThe TaskFlow Team"

#### Guidelines
- Acknowledge the issue clearly
- Provide step-by-step solutions
- Reference documentation links when applicable
- Set expectations for follow-up if needed
- Auto-acknowledgment sent immediately: "We've received your request and will respond within [SLA time]."

#### Example
```
Hello Maria,

Thank you for contacting TaskFlow Support. I understand you invited 5 team members but they haven't received the invitation emails.

This can sometimes happen due to email filtering rules. Here's what I recommend:

1. Ask your team members to check their spam/junk folders and add "noreply@techcorp.com" to their safe senders list.
2. I can resend the invitations from our end. Would you like me to do that now?
3. Alternatively, you can generate new invite links from the "Team" tab → Click the dropdown next to each member → "Resend Invite."

If the issue persists after trying these steps, please reply with the email addresses you invited, and I'll investigate further.

Thank you for your patience!

Best regards,
The TaskFlow Team
```

## Do's and Don'ts

### ✅ Do
- Acknowledge the customer's emotion: "I understand this is frustrating..."
- Use the customer's name throughout the conversation
- Provide clear, actionable steps
- Confirm resolution: "Did that work for you?"
- Offer proactive help: "You might also want to..."
- Apologize sincerely when TechCorp is at fault

### ❌ Don't
- Use technical jargon without explanation
- Say "I don't know" — instead: "Let me look into that for you"
- Blame the customer: "You did X wrong" → "Here's what might have happened"
- Over-promise: "This will never happen again" → "We're working to prevent this"
- Use negative language: "You can't do that" → "Here's what you can do instead"
- Ignore the emotional tone of the customer's message

## Handling Difficult Situations

### Angry Customer
```
I completely understand your frustration, [Name]. This isn't the experience we want for you, and I'm here to make it right. Let me look into this immediately and get back to you within [timeframe].
```

### Confused Customer
```
No worries at all, [Name]! This can be a bit tricky at first. Let me break it down step by step for you...
```

### Customer Threatening to Churn
```
I'm really sorry to hear that, [Name]. Your experience matters a lot to us. I'm escalating this to our Customer Success team who can work with you directly. You'll hear from them within [SLA time].
```

### Customer Asking for Feature We Don't Have
```
That's a great suggestion, [Name]! While we don't have that feature right now, I've logged your feedback with our Product team. In the meantime, here's a workaround that might help...
```

## Language & Tone Quick Reference

| Situation | Recommended Tone | Example Phrase |
|-----------|------------------|----------------|
| Greeting | Warm, personalized | "Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out!" |
| Acknowledging issue | Empathetic, validating | "I can see why that would be frustrating." |
| Providing solution | Clear, confident | "Here's how we can fix this..." |
| Setting expectations | Transparent, honest | "This may take up to [time]. I'll keep you updated." |
| Closing | Helpful, open-ended | "Is there anything else I can help with?" |
| Apologizing | Sincere, accountable | "I apologize for the inconvenience. Here's what we're doing..." |

## Internal Notes
- All responses should align with TechCorp's core values: **Customer First, Transparency, Innovation, Reliability**
- When in doubt, default to **Email** tone (semi-formal) as the baseline
- Review and update these guidelines quarterly based on customer feedback and brand evolution
